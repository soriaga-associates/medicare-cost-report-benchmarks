# Medicare cost report benchmarks

Open-source tool and dataset from [Soriaga & Associates, LLC](https://www.soriagacpa.com). It turns the public Medicare cost reports filed by **hospices** (Form CMS-1984-14) and **home health agencies** (Form CMS-1728-20) into aggregate benchmarks: cost per day or per visit, level-of-care and payer mix, size, and operating margins, by year, state, size band and type of control.

Every Medicare-certified hospice and home health agency files a cost report with CMS each year. CMS publishes the data, but the raw files are large and hard to use. This project cleans them with documented rules and publishes only aggregates. No provider names, CCNs, addresses or provider-level rows appear in any output.

Benchmark pages, with commentary:

- Hospice: https://www.soriagacpa.com/hospice-cost-report-benchmarks
- Home health: https://www.soriagacpa.com/home-health-cost-report-benchmarks

> General information from public CMS filings, not an audit, valuation, or advice for any agency.

## What you get

Committed in `output/` and regenerated by `build`:

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `output/hospice/benchmarks.json` and `.csv` | Hospice benchmarks, FY 2015 to 2025 |
| `output/home-health/benchmarks.json` and `.csv` | Home health benchmarks, CMS file years 2020 to 2024 |
| `output/SUMMARY.md` | National medians for the latest complete year, and the trend |

The JSON has three parts: `meta` (credit, license, sources, year definition, per-year counts of every exclusion, notes), `metrics` (id, label, unit, definition and the exact worksheet line or CMS column each uses) and `rows`. The CSV holds the same rows, with one comment line of credit first, then a header.

Each row is one cell: `group_type` (`national`, `state`, `size`, `control`), `group`, `year`, `metric`, `n`, `p25`, `median`, `p75`, `mean`, `pooled`, `suppressed`. Cells with fewer than 11 agencies have `suppressed: true`, `n` shown and all statistics empty.

`pooled` is filled only for share metrics (hospice share of days at each level, hospice Medicare share of days, home health Medicare share of visits) and for the hospice `agencies_providing_*` metrics; it is empty for all other metrics. For a share metric it is the sum of the numerators divided by the sum of the denominators across the agencies in the cell (for example total Medicare days over total days), which weights large agencies more and shows small levels of care that per-agency medians round to 0.0%. For `agencies_providing_chc`, `_irc` and `_gip` it is the fraction of agencies with more than zero days at that level, and the quartiles and mean are empty; `n` is the number of agencies. The same privacy floor applies to pooled values.

## Quick start

Requires Python 3.11 or later. No runtime dependencies (standard library only).

```
pip install -e .
python -m crbench download          # about 180 MB, cached in data/raw/ (git-ignored)
python -m crbench build             # writes output/
python -m crbench info              # shows cached files and built outputs
```

`build --provider hospice` or `--provider home-health` builds one form. `download --force` fetches everything again. Downloads are polite: one request at a time, a 2 second pause between requests, retries with backoff, and a User-Agent that identifies this project. Downloaded files are treated as data only; nothing from them is ever executed. To run the tests: `pip install -e .[dev]` then `pytest`. The tests use synthetic fixtures only.

## Year definitions

- **Hospice: federal fiscal year of the cost reporting period's begin date.** FY 2023 means periods beginning 10/1/2022 through 9/30/2023. The year is computed from the begin date in the `rpt` file, not from the file name. (In the 2015 to 2026 files the two agree for every report; the build would record any that differ as `fy_differs_from_file_year` in `raw_data_checks`.)
- **Home health: CMS's data.cms.gov file year.** Each yearly CSV mostly holds reports whose period begins in that calendar year (in the 2024 file, about 95% begin in 2024 and the rest begin late in 2023). It is not a uniform fiscal year, and agencies with non-calendar fiscal years fall in the file that CMS assigned them. Read "2024" as "CMS's 2024 file".

### Field map

| Days: continuous home care (CHC), routine home care (RHC), inpatient respite (IRC), general inpatient (GIP), total | S-1 Part II (`S100000`) | 30, 31, 32, 33, 34 | 4 (total); Medicare is column 1 of line 34 |

### Cleaning rules

Rules are applied in this order within each year. The counts are in `meta.counts` of each JSON file.

1. **No financial data or no costs or days.** Hospice: Worksheet C line 21 col 3 or line 22 col 3 missing or zero or negative. Home health: a blank `Total Cost` is counted as "no financial data" (about a third of rows); a `Total Cost` or `Total, Total Visits` that is zero, negative or blank (with cost present) is counted as "no cost or days".
2. **Partial-year filter.** Keep only periods of 300 to 430 days (about 10 to 14 months). Shorter and longer periods, and reports whose dates cannot be read, are excluded as "partial-year".
3. **One report per provider per year.** Keep the longest period; on a tie keep the highest `rpt_rec_num`. The count of drops is `dropped_duplicates`. Because rules 1 and 2 run first, this rule dropped 0 reports in the current data: in every year, no CCN had two reports that both had financial data and a 300 to 430 day period. `raw_rows_sharing_a_ccn` shows how many raw rows shared a CCN with another row (for example 176 in the 2024 home health file, 88 in hospice FY 2024); nearly all are a short or empty companion to a full report.
4. **Range guard for margins.** Margins are computed only where net patient revenue is above zero (or Medicare PPS payments are above zero, for the Medicare margin). Margins below -100% or above +100% are treated as data errors and excluded from the margin metric only. Counted as `range_excluded` under `metric_screening`.
5. **Percentile screen.** For every metric and year (except the share and `agencies_providing_*` metrics, which are bounded ratios and are not trimmed, so that their pooled values use every cleaned agency), values below the 1st or above the 99th percentile of the national values (`statistics.quantiles`, n=100, inclusive method) are excluded from that metric only. The cut points and counts are in `metric_screening`. The screen is skipped for a metric-year with fewer than 100 values.
6. **Privacy floor.** Any cell (group, year, metric) with fewer than 11 agencies is suppressed: `n` is shown, the statistics are empty, and `suppressed` is true. The "Other" type-of-control group in home health therefore usually shows only as suppressed.
7. **Completeness.** A year is marked `complete: false` when its kept-report count is below 90% of the prior year's. `meta.latest_complete_year` is the newest complete year.

Statistics are `n`, the 25th percentile, median, 75th percentile (`statistics.quantiles(n=4, method="inclusive")`) and the mean.

Reports in states other than the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico, and reports with an unrecognized or blank type of control, count in the national group only.

### Metric definitions

Hospice

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `cost_per_day_total` | The average cost per diem **as filed** (Worksheet C line 23 col 3). Only if the filed value is missing or not above zero is it recomputed as line 21 divided by line 22 (counted as `per_diem_total_recomputed`). Filed and recomputed values that differ by more than the larger of $0.05 or 0.1% are counted as `per_diem_total_differs_from_recomputed`; in the current data there are none. |
| `cost_per_day_chc`, `_rhc`, `_irc`, `_gip` | The per diem as filed on Worksheet C lines 3, 8, 13 and 18 (col 3), only for agencies reporting days at that level; recomputed from the level's cost and days only if the filed value is missing. |
| `share_days_chc`, `_rhc`, `_irc`, `_gip` | Level days (S-1 lines 30 to 33 col 4) divided by total days (line 34 col 4), per agency; a blank level counts as zero days. Quartiles and mean are across agencies; `pooled` is total level days over total days. |
| `agencies_providing_chc`, `_irc`, `_gip` | Share of agencies with more than zero days at that level (S-1 lines 30, 32, 33 col 4). Published in `pooled` only. |
| `medicare_share_days` | S-1 line 34 col 1 divided by line 34 col 4, per agency; a blank Medicare count is treated as zero. `pooled` is total Medicare days over total days. |
| `operating_margin` | (F-2 line 8 col 4 minus line 41 col 2) divided by line 8 col 4, all payers. |
| `total_patient_days` | S-1 line 34 col 4 (falls back to Worksheet C line 22 col 3 if missing). Also the size-band variable. |

Home health

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `cost_per_visit_*` | The reported average cost per visit for each of 9 disciplines, only where above zero. |
| `total_visits` | `Total, Total Visits`. Also the size-band variable. |
| `medicare_share_visits` | Medicare Title XVIII visits divided by total visits, per agency; a blank Medicare count is treated as zero. `pooled` is total Medicare visits over total visits. |
| `operating_margin` | (F-1 line 3 minus F-1 line 17) divided by F-1 line 3, all payers. |
| `medicare_margin_cost_based` | (Medicare PPS payments minus total HHA Medicare program cost) divided by Medicare PPS payments, where payments are the sum of six columns (see below). Not the MedPAC margin: it ignores bad debts and sequestration. |

Size bands: hospice by total days and home health by total visits: under 5,000; 5,000-19,999; 20,000-49,999; 50,000+. Type of control groups: for-profit (proprietary), nonprofit, government, and other.

### Type-of-control codes

The codes differ by form, so each form has its own table (checked against PRM 15-2 chapter 43).

| Group | Hospice codes | Home health codes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nonprofit | 1, 2 | 1, 2, 6 |
| For-profit (proprietary) | 3, 4, 5, 6 | 3, 4, 5 |
| Government | 7 to 13 | 8 to 13 |
| Other | none | 7 (governmental and private combined) |

For hospice, the code is taken from S-1 alpha line 19 col 1. In the 2023 file it matches the `PRVDR_CTRL_TYPE_CD` in the `rpt` file for all 4,996 reports.

### Worksheet F-2 Part II: which column

The cost report form lays out Part II (lines 27 to 42, expenses and net income) with four payer columns, but in the HCRIS data these lines are stored in **column 2 only**. We checked every report in every year file (FY 2015 to 2026): for lines 27, 41 and 42, column 2 was the only populated column in 100% of reports that have the line, and no report had values in more than one column. The build counts this per year under `raw_data_checks` (`f2_line41_columns_2` and so on), so a future change would show up. The rule used: take column 2; if it is absent, take column 4; if more than one column is populated, still take column 2 and never sum. Columns are never added together.

Consistency check: net income (line 42) should equal total revenues (F-2 line 26 col 4) minus total operating expenses (line 41), within $1 or 0.1%. In the current data it passes for every report where all three lines are present (for example 5,313 of 5,316 reports in FY 2024; the other 3 lack one of the lines). The counts are `f2_net_income_check_pass`, `_fail` and `_not_possible` in `raw_data_checks`.

### Home health Medicare payments and outliers

Medicare PPS payments for the cost-based margin are the sum of **all six** columns: the four `Total PPS Payment` episode columns (Worksheet D lines 10 to 13: full episodes without outliers, full episodes with outliers, LUPA, PEP) and the two `Total PPS Outlier Payment` columns (lines 14 and 15).

Evidence: Provider Reimbursement Manual 15-2 section 4715.2 (https://www.cms.gov/files/document/r7p247i.pdf) says lines 10 to 13 hold the PPS payment for each episode or period category and that the PPS outlier payments are entered separately on lines 14 and 15, and CMS's electronic cost report edits sum Worksheet D Part II column 1 lines 10 through 15. The outlier lines are therefore additive and not contained in line 11. The data.cms.gov data dictionary alone does not say this; it describes the columns without stating whether line 11 includes outliers. Consistent with the manual, in the 2024 file the line 14 outlier payment exceeds the line 11 payment in 414 of 7,473 reports with financial data, which could not happen if line 11 contained it. The outlier columns add about 2.4% to payments in that file.

## Limitations

- Cost reports are provider-filed and may be unaudited.
- HCRIS keeps the highest status per report, so the data mixes as-submitted, settled and reopened reports.
- All-payer margins include costs Medicare does not allow, and are not the same as Medicare margins.
- Partial-year filers (periods under 300 or over 430 days) are excluded.
- The newest year is incomplete and fills in over time. Hospice FY 2025 is below 90% of FY 2024 and is flagged incomplete. Its rows are published but should not be compared with complete years.
- Figures are not risk-adjusted or wage-adjusted.
- Home health rows with no financial data (blank `Total Cost`, about a third of rows) are excluded, so home health benchmarks describe agencies that filed financial data.
- Hospice covers freestanding hospices filing Form CMS-1984-14 only. Hospital-, SNF- and HHA-based hospices file other cost report forms and are not included.
- Many margins are data errors: each year roughly 4 to 7% of hospice margins and 3 to 5% of home health margins fall outside -100% to +100% and are excluded, so margin medians describe agencies with plausible filings.
- Home health years are CMS file years, not a uniform fiscal year.

## Validation

- **Raw cross-check (home health).** A random sample of 8 reports from the cleaned home health CSV was compared with the raw HCRIS HHA20 data for FY 2024 (Worksheet C column 4, lines 1 and 3). All 8 of 8 matched exactly. Reviewer check, 2026-10-22.
- **Independent recomputation.** A reviewer recomputed national medians separately from the raw files and matched this build: hospice FY 2024 routine home care cost per day $153.73 and all-payer margin 2.07%; home health 2024 RN cost per visit $232.25 against $232.26 here (one report sits at the 1st/99th percentile trim edge) and operating margin 4.03%. The reviewer's median of the filed hospice cost per day (Worksheet C line 23) was $159.04, which is what this build publishes.
- **F-2 Part II column.** Lines 27, 41 and 42 are stored in column 2 only, in every report in every year (FY 2015 to 2026); see above.
- **Net income check.** Line 42 equals line 26 minus line 41 within $1 or 0.1% for every report where all three lines are present: 0 failures in every year (`f2_net_income_check_fail` never appears in `raw_data_checks`).
- **Rule order.** No-data rows are removed first, then partial-year periods, then duplicates. This order was reviewed and approved.
- **Automated tests.** `pytest` runs synthetic-fixture tests of the worksheet mapping, each cleaning rule, the privacy floor, the pooled values, and the output schema.
